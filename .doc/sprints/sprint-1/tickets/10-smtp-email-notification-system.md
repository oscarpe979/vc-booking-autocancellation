# 10: Corporate SMTP Email Notification Engine & Template System

**What to build:** An outbound email notification engine decoupling message delivery behind the Hexagonal `INotificationDriver` port (defined in ticket 01). The adapter layer implements `SmtpNotificationAdapter` connecting to corporate internal smart hosts (default: `outlook.suncorp.com.au:25` as an unauthenticated direct relay) and `MockNotificationDriver` for zero-dependency local laptop development with rich terminal logging and in-memory capture. This ticket is the single owner of the warning email gate rules (LLD §5.8) that tickets 02, 03, and 06 apply. When a warning deadline is fixed, the service sends one warning email for that deadline, stating the real time remaining to the fixed deadline (`promptExpiresAt`, which includes the grace period in `LIVE`) and the release time in the codec-local clock timezone. If the email fails or the organizer email is missing, the prompt stays visible but automatic release is blocked for the rest of the lifecycle. With `NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED = false`, no warning email is sent and the gate does not block release. Upon an automatic release, the service dispatches a differentiated cancellation notice distinguishing No-Show from Early Departure. In `DRY_RUN`, real emails are suppressed and structured `[DRY RUN - WOULD_SEND_EMAIL]` logs are emitted; `MONITOR_ONLY` rooms never call the notification driver. SMTP settings (`SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_FROM`, `SMTP_SECURE`, `NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED`) are part of `FleetSettings` and manageable at runtime from the `/admin` dashboard.

- [ ] `MONITOR_ONLY` rooms never call the notification driver and emit no email logs
